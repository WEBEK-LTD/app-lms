# Yallapp Community, phase 1: area E's app screens (never merged)

Real screenshots of the Flutter app (`community/`) at 390 × 844 points, 2x, drawn by the widget tests with the app's own fonts
(`YC_SHOTS_DIR=<dir> flutter test test/features`), from `wip-community-app-features`.

- `compare/question-dark-en__mockup-left_app-right.jpg`: the approved design's dark question phone (mobile.html, cropped exactly from `yallapp-community-mobile.png`) beside the app's. Approved additions: the question's own text and vote under the asker, the answer bar above the tab bar, and (for the asker only) an outline-green Accept on unaccepted answers. `question-dark-ar` is the same in Arabic.
- `app/`: every area-E screen. The Q&A tab, a question (dark, light, Arabic), asking with similar questions, Learn, a video, an article and a locked tutorial, the Gold verification form (form, rejected, granted in Arabic), notifications (light, dark), search.

Shadows are drawn blurred as on a phone (the test's solid shadows are off for each shot's frame). Icons are Material Symbols Rounded.
