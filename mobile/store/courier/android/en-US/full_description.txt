Yallapp Courier is the app for couriers of the delivery companies that work with Yallapp. Your company adds your mobile number or email to its couriers; you sign in with a code and start delivering.

- One sign-in for every company you deliver for. Pick one and its jobs open; switch company from Account at any time.
- Go on and off duty. Off duty, you're not offered new jobs.
- Your jobs, the jobs waiting for a courier and the ones you've done.
- Each job's steps: picked up, on the way, delivered, or couldn't deliver with a reason. Give a job back when you can't take it.
- The cash to collect, shown on the job.
- Call the sender or the receiver, and get directions in your maps app.
- While a parcel is on the way and the job is open on your screen, its customer sees where it is. Nothing is shared when the app is closed.
- Notifications for new jobs and changes to yours, opening the right company's job.

No company yet? Ask the company you deliver for to add your number or email to its couriers, then sign in again.
