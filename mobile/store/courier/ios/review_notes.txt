PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Yallapp (yallapp.com.tr) is a public service where small businesses make their own branded mobile app without code; delivery companies among them run their deliveries on it. Yallapp Courier is the courier app for every delivery company on Yallapp that chooses it: a company adds a courier's mobile number or email to its couriers, and the courier signs in with a one-time code and works on that company's deliveries. The app is not made for one company, and has no sign-up: couriers are added by their company.

The app sells nothing: no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no payment screens and no ads. Couriers see the cash to collect from the receiver, which they collect by hand.

HOW TO USE IT
On the sign-in screen, enter the review mobile number (or choose Email and enter the review email) given in the sign-in details, tap Send code, and enter the code given there. No message is sent to that number. You'll see a demo delivery company with a sample job: open it from My jobs, follow its steps (Picked up, On the way, Delivered), call or get directions. It reaches no real customer or courier.

Account > Switch company goes back to the list of companies; Account > Log out signs out of the app and every company on the phone. Account > Delete my Yallapp Courier account deletes the account (also under the list of companies): every phone is signed out and every company session ends.

Location: the app asks for the location only while a parcel is on the way and its job is open on the screen, so the customer can see where it is. It never uses the location in the background. Notifications: new jobs and changes to the courier's jobs.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
Yallapp's own platform at yallapp.com.tr (API, sign-in, data) and Firebase Cloud Messaging with Apple Push Notification service for notifications. Directions open the phone's own maps app. No payment processors, no advertising or analytics SDKs, no third-party sign-in.

REGIONS
The app works the same in all regions, in English, Arabic and Turkish. What a courier sees depends only on the companies that added them.

REGULATED INDUSTRIES
None. The app processes no payments and holds no health or financial data.
