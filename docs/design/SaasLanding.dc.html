Questions

Do I need my own Apple and Google developer accounts?

Yes. Your apps are published under your own name. Apple charges a yearly fee and Google charges a one-time fee.

Which LMS plugins are supported?

Tutor LMS, LearnDash, LearnPress, LifterLMS, MasterStudy LMS, Sensei LMS and Masteriyo, in their free and pro editions, with current Digits versions. The plugin detects what's installed and adapts.

Can students buy courses inside the app?

On Android, through your site's existing checkout. On iOS, the app is for watching enrolled courses, in line with Apple's rules.

What if my lessons contain custom HTML or code?

Standard content is shown natively. Complex content such as embeds or page builders is shown exactly like your website.
