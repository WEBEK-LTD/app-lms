# Photo credits

Every photo in these screens is a real photo under CC0 or in the public domain,
taken from Wikimedia Commons. None needs attribution; they are credited here
anyway. The files are the repo's own copies (`platform/web/public/…`), already
used by Zad's demo; the screens reference them in place and nothing was copied.

Icons are Material Symbols Rounded (Google, Apache License 2.0). Fonts are the
repo's: Plus Jakarta Sans, Lexend, Readex Pro, JetBrains Mono, Bricolage
Grotesque and IBM Plex Sans Arabic (SIL Open Font License 1.1).

The map in 05b is drawn by hand in SVG: no map tiles or map provider.
