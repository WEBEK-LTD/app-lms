# Yallapp Community: store listing (draft)

Roadmap 231. A draft for the App Store and Google Play: nothing is uploaded
or published until the owner approves it. The screenshots are on
`wip-community-shots-app` under `store/` (6.9" iPhone, 1320 × 2868;
Android phone, 1080 × 1920). The privacy answers are in
`privacy-answers.md` and the reviewers' notes in `review-notes.md`.

The listing says nothing about prices or buying. The app sells nothing;
the copy only mentions that some tutorials are unlocked by the member's
account.

## The app's identity

| | |
| --- | --- |
| App name (both stores, at most 30) | Yallapp Community |
| Bundle id / package | `tr.com.yallapp.community` |
| Primary category | App Store: Education · Google Play: Education |
| Secondary category (App Store) | Social Networking |
| Languages | English (default), Arabic, Turkish |
| Support URL | https://community.yallapp.com.tr (to confirm: area F's page) |
| Privacy policy URL | https://community.yallapp.com.tr/privacy (to confirm: the page must exist before submitting) |

Why Education: the app is mostly questions, answers and official tutorials
for people who build apps. The social parts (following, friends) serve that.
If the owner prefers Social Networking as the primary category, the copy
still fits.

| Unrestricted web access | No: links open in the phone's browser, outside the app |
